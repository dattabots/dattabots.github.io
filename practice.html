Practice areas

Two disciplines. One standard of care.

The experienced team at WK Legal has a broad understanding of all parts of the real estate realm, including the purchasing, offering, renting and financing of residential and commercial properties.

// 01 · Real Estate

Buying. Selling. Leasing. Financing.

WK Legal are specialists in real estate and work with a multitude of private and business loan specialists, from large global banks to small private funds, along with contract brokers, investors, developers, lenders and other attorneys.

We represent individuals, investors and foreign nationals in the purchase, sale and leasing of residential, commercial and mixed-use properties, from a single-unit co-op or condo to a multi-million dollar townhouse or multi-family home.

  • Construction contracts
  • Purchase and sales agreements
  • Closings
  • Tax issues
  • Titles and insurance
  • 1031 exchanges
  • Zoning compliance
  • Residential and commercial leasing

// 02 · Banking

Closing counsel for lenders.

We serve residential and commercial mortgage lenders. In residential real estate the firm reports some 2,000 transactions completed during the past 2 years; lending clients include Wells Fargo, Citibank, TD Bank, Citizens Bank, Gibraltar Bank and Guardhill Financial.

In commercial real estate we represent borrowers and lenders in construction, term, permanent and mezzanine loans, including loans intended for securitization or syndication. Lending transactions follow federal law including RESPA, TILA, FCRA, ECOA and the Fair Housing Act, plus New York and New Jersey regulations.

  • Salability projections
  • Collateral exchanges and payoffs
  • Foreclosure and evictions
  • Title curing
  • First mortgages (conventional and jumbos)
  • Apartment, FHA and SONYMA loans
  • Home equity and realty-anchored business loans
  • Settlement agent: attorney review of documents, title and survey; closing documents, representation at closing
